## NCR

NCR has no citymun-grain source polygon at all in the per-province files (`docs/BOUNDARY_RECONCILIATION.md`: all 17 of its HUC-as-province rows are missing) — the source instead files the region as 4 old PSA-style administrative groupings, each spanning several of today's cities (verified against the source: e.g. its "Second District" polygon is one dissolved shape covering Mandaluyong + Marikina + Pasig + Quezon City + San Juan). Those 4 polygons are **not** used as district polygons — none of today's 32 NCR congressional districts corresponds to one of them, and guessing that correspondence is exactly what docs/LEGISLATIVE_DISTRICTS_PLAN.md §7 guardrail 1 rules out.

What the 4 grouping files *do* carry, fetched at their own citymun grain (`municities-provdist-<code>`), is one polygon per constituent city — the citymun source NCR is otherwise missing entirely. Those are pulled out and exact-matched (case-insensitive) against `dim_geo`'s NCR citymun rows, which is how NCR's whole-city, single-seat districts below got a polygon: 16 of NCR's 16 non-Manila cities matched this way. Unmatched source names: ['City of Manila'] — "City of Manila" is expected here, since Manila has no single whole-city `dim_geo` citymun row (its own PSGC children are its ten sub-city districts instead, e.g. Tondo, Quiapo — none of which has a source polygon at any grain, so Manila's own district split stays unresolved).
